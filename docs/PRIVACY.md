# What students should know (paste into Canvas)

**AI George Live** is an AI coach for this course. It is not Dr. Antoniou and it is not a live person. It looks and sounds like him because he recorded his face and voice for it.

- **It runs on your computer.** The AI model, speech recognition and voice all run inside your browser. Your microphone audio is turned into text on your computer and is not recorded or uploaded. Your camera is only a self-view and is used on your computer to notice when you're there. Nothing is sent to Dr. Antoniou, Lynn University or any company.
- **Conversations aren't saved.** Closing the tab erases them.
- **First start downloads the AI** (about 1–2.5 GB, once). Use Wi-Fi.
- **Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.** On a phone, use the text coach link instead.
- **It's a coach, not a grader.** It explains concepts and reacts to your reasoning. It won't grade anything or write or solve graded work, and it can be wrong. Verify what it says, follow the course AI-use disclosure, and treat Canvas as the official source for deadlines and requirements.
- **Keep the passphrase within the class.** It unlocks the course material and the coach's face and voice.
